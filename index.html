About this Project

The concept: to let people experience physics, not only receive it.

Three modes, three steps of the scientific method

AbductionShow Me · What could explain this?
InductionBeginner · Does the evidence support it?
DeductionExpert · What must follow if it is true?

Light upon Light is a collection of interactive experiments in electromagnetism, quantum physics and optics. Each one is computed from the governing equations, in SI units, and modelled on an experiment that was really performed. The aim is not only to present established results, but to let the visitor experience physics: to observe something surprising, to ask what could explain it, to test the guess, and only then to meet the equations.

Science moves through three steps of the scientific method: abduction proposes an explanation, induction tests it against evidence, and deduction works out exactly what must follow from it. Abduction, the step that creates new ideas, is the one most often missing when science is taught. The three modes of every project follow these three steps, in this order: Show Me, Beginner, Expert.

Show Me: abduction, the heart of the project

“I’m Schrödinger’s cat” and “I’m Maxwell’s Demon” are narrated films. Before every experiment they tell where the idea came from: the puzzle someone noticed and the guess they made. Then the experiment plays, pausing on a question before each result, and it ends with what the scientists thought, expected and argued about. Watching, wondering and guessing come before any formula. The intention is to create the questions, and it is recommended even for experts.

Beginner: induction

Simple controls and “think first, then check” clue cards: each card asks you to predict what will happen, then sets up the experiment so you can see whether nature agrees. You test your ideas against what the screen and the measurements show, and build intuition from evidence. The intention is to test the questions.

Expert: deduction

Every parameter, the full equations, live values in SI units, the numerical method and its limits. Here you start from the laws and derive what must happen, then check it quantitatively. The intention is to make the ideas exact.

Beyond electromagnetism and the quantum

The first two projects explore electromagnetism and quantum physics. The same approach, idea first, then experiment, will carry on into non-linear optics, crystals and materials, laser and ultrafast physics, and Fourier optics.

What is inside every project

  • Clue cards: “predict, then check” questions that set up the experiment for you.
  • Where the idea came from, and what they thought: the origin of every idea before the experiment, and the scientists’ own expectations, arguments and criticism after it.
  • References: the original papers and the real experiments behind each step.
  • A real experiment, not just a simulation: each scene reproduces an experiment that was actually carried out, with the real numbers, and the physics is solved from the equations, not animated by hand.
  • Sound: every action has its own sound, the detected particles ring like a chord, and the films have their own music, all generated live in the browser.

The emblem

The emblem is a real calculation, not a drawing. Two point sources emit the whole visible spectrum in phase. Where their waves arrive together, light adds to light (constructive interference, the white central fringe). Where they arrive half a wavelength apart, they cancel (destructive interference, the dark bands). Because each colour has its own wavelength, the higher-order fringes split into spectra.
