About

Light upon Light is a home for interactive physics: visual, hands-on explanations of optics and electromagnetism that you can explore in the browser, with correct physics and SI units.

The emblem is a real calculation, not a drawing. Two point sources emit the whole visible spectrum in phase. Where their waves arrive together, light adds to light (constructive interference, the white central fringe). Where they arrive half a wavelength apart, they cancel (destructive interference, the dark bands). Because each colour has its own wavelength, the higher-order fringes split into spectra.
